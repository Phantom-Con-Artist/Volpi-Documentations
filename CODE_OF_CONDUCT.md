# Community guidelines

Volpi is made for researchers, and its community should feel like a good seminar room: curious, patient and kind. These guidelines apply in the Volpi Discord, in this repository's issues and pull requests, and anywhere else you represent the Volpi community.

## What we ask

- **Be kind and patient.** People come from different fields, countries and levels of experience. Many are under deadline pressure. Assume good intent.
- **Be specific.** Clear questions and clear reports help everyone, including the next person with the same problem.
- **Keep research private.** Do not share unpublished work, participant data or personal details, your own or anyone else's.
- **Credit others.** If someone's answer, tip or extension helped you, say so.
- **Stay on topic.** Keep discussion about Volpi, research workflows and related tools. Use the right Discord channel when there is one.

## What is not allowed

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual content, or unwanted attention of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic posting.
- Pirated papers, software or other material.

## If something goes wrong

If someone breaks these guidelines, or you feel unsafe, tell a moderator or the maintainer privately on Discord. You can also report a message to Discord or content to GitHub using their own tools. Reports are kept confidential.

Moderators may remove content, give a warning, or remove someone from the community, depending on what happened. The aim is a community where everyone can ask a question without fear of being mocked for it.
