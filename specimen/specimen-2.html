Most research lives in five places at once. Volpi keeps it in one folder, with papers, notes, data and drafts side by side, linked to each other.

I.

Reading

Highlight a PDF in four colours and write in its margin. The original file is never changed.1

1 Highlights and notes are kept in a small file beside the PDF.

II.

Linking

Link a note to a paper, a page or a figure. Rename any of them and the links follow.2

2 Undo puts every renamed link back in one step.

III.

Analysing

Open a CSV and see its gaps and outliers first. Then chart it and keep the figure.3

3 Charts are saved as SVG, ready for a paper.

IV.

Writing

Write with numbered sections, figures and citations from your own library. Export to Word or PDF.4

4 APA, IEEE, MLA, Chicago, Harvard and Vancouver.
