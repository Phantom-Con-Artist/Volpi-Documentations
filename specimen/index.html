No. 01

Three small promises.

i.

Files you own.

Each project is an ordinary folder. Notes are Markdown. Highlights sit beside the PDF.

ii.

Links that hold.

Rename a paper and every note that points to it follows. Undo puts it back.

iii.

A calm desk.

Day and Night themes, Zen mode and a focus timer. Nothing pings you.

No. 02

How a day goes.

Read

Mark the page.

Highlight a PDF and write in its margin. The original file is never changed.

Link

Follow the thread.

Link a note to a paper, a page or a figure. The graph shows where they meet.

Analyse

Look at the numbers.

Open a CSV and see its gaps and outliers. Chart it and keep the figure as SVG.

Write

Put it into words.

Cite from your own library as you write. Export to Word or PDF.
